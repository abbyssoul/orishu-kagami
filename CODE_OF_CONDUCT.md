# Code of conduct

Participation in Orishu Kagami should be safe, constructive, and focused on the
work. Treat contributors with respect regardless of experience, identity,
background, or point of view.

Acceptable participation includes evidence-based technical disagreement,
patient questions, clear review feedback, and acknowledging mistakes.
Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate
humiliation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's
private contact channel. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the
community. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when representing the project in
public community spaces.
